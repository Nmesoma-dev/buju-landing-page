About BNXN

Singer . Songwriter . Afro-Fusion Artist

BNXN (also known as Buju) is a 26-year-old Afro-fusion singer/songwriter from Lagos, Nigeria. He began gaining recognition in the Nigerian music industry with his unique blend of Afro-fusion, reggae, and contemporary R&B.


Buju first gained widespread attention in 2019 with his hit single "Energy," earning him a spot as one of the rising stars in Nigerian music. In 2020, Buju released another hit single titled "Lenu," which further solidified his position as an emerging talent. The song became a fan favorite, garnering millions of streams and establishing him as a force to be reckoned with in the Afrobeat scene.

His talent and hard work have earned Buju nominations for several awards, including The Headies Award for Next Rated in 2021. With his distinct sound and rising popularity, Buju continues to make waves and is poised to achieve even greater success in the Nigerian music industry and beyond.
